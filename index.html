§ I — The frame

Three seats. Sixteen candidates.
One ward.

Bunhill ward elects three councillors. You get three votes — but you don't have to use all three; they can be split across parties. The current three Bunhill councillors were all elected as Labour in 2022. One of them, Phil Graham, was suspended from Labour in May 2024 for backing Jeremy Corbyn's independent campaign in Islington North; he formally resigned on 25 July 2024 over the two-child benefit cap vote, and stands again here as an Islington Community Independent.

Labour has run Islington since 2010 and won 48 of 51 seats in 2022. PollCheck's 2026 projection has Labour holding power but losing roughly 16 seats — almost all to a surging Green Party that's projected to grow from 3 seats to ~20. Bunhill is not in the top-five marginal wards, but turnout effects in a low-turnout ward can still produce surprises. — PollCheck projections, 2026

Labour · five missions

Incumbent · 44 / 51 current seats · Cllr Una O'Halloran (Leader since Nov 2024)

The five missions

Conservatives · eleven pledges

0 current seats · projected ~5% · LTN-removal is the signature policy
Expand

The eleven pledges

  • Scrap all of Islington's LTNs (signature, "central" policy)
  • Cut Council Tax by 5%
  • End the Right-to-Buy buyback programme; reallocate budget to repairs of lifts, heating, CCTV
  • Insulate all council housing; solar on all new social housing
  • Cut affordable target on new developments from 50% to 35%
  • "Innocent until proven guilty" landlord enforcement; helpline for "accidental" landlords
  • "Bobbies on Bikes" patrol model; bleed kits on main streets
  • Use Government Safer Streets Fund for lighting and CCTV
  • Repurpose schools / community / leisure space for youth; "Your Future" careers from Y7
  • Smart-bin estate refuse collection; restore free bulky-item collection
  • Free parking for less able-bodied residents; targeted business rate help

Tone

  • "Business-like" review of all council spending
  • Argues Islington's council tax is roughly 2× Wandsworth / Westminster
  • Argues £89m of own funds spent on Right-to-Buy buybacks "moves homes between categories" without adding stock

Reform UK · no local manifesto

0 current seats · projected ~8% · national policy applies
Expand

Reform UK have not issued a local Islington manifesto. Voters effectively get the national platform as proxy: hard-line on illegal immigration, large tax cuts, tougher criminal justice, rollback of net-zero policy, cost-of-living focus.

Local Reform candidates in Islington tend to overlap with Conservative campaign lines on council tax, LTNs and crime, while emphasising a "wasteful spending" narrative.

Caveat. Of the seven groupings on the Bunhill ballot, Reform UK has the least transparent local platform. No public local biographies for the Bunhill candidates were located.

§ VI — The polling stations

Where you actually vote

Three polling stations serve Bunhill ward. Polls open 07:00, close 22:00 sharp. Photo ID is required. Your poll card tells you which station — they're assigned by register range.

Accepted ID includes UK passport, UK / EEA driving licence, Older Person's bus pass, Blue Badge, biometric immigration document and the free Voter Authority Certificate. If yours has expired, it can still be accepted as long as you are still recognisable from the photograph.
