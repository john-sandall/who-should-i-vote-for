# Green Party — Islington 2026

**Status:** Currently 3 councillors (the official opposition alongside ICI's "Independent & Green Group"). Polling projects ~20 seats in 2026 — biggest swing of any party.
**Manifesto:** "Islington Green Party 2026" (full PDF + Easy Read version).
**Borough lead:** Cllr Benali Hamdache (Leader of the Green Group on Islington Council; Leader of the joint Independent & Green Group).

## Three priorities

1. **Homes for people, not profit**
2. **Making Islington affordable**
3. **Clean air, safe streets, healthy lives**

> "For the last twelve years one political party has dominated Islington Council. At the last three elections, Labour won all of the seats in every ward except one. This has been unhealthy for democracy in Islington."
> — Foreword by Cllr Benali Hamdache

## Headline pledges

### Housing — "homes for people, not profit"
- Prioritise **public and community-led housing** (Community Land Trusts)
- Oppose giving up public land for unaffordable luxury homes (Greens claim 18 new private homes are being built for every 1 council home under Labour)
- Prioritise brownfield redevelopment; *protect* community gardens, playgrounds and green spaces
- Fix broken lifts and persistent disrepair; install proper fire safety plans
- Plan and deliver a council-housing **retrofit programme** (insulation, lower heating bills, lower emissions)
- Extend **landlord licensing across the entire borough** for all privately rented properties
- Implement Mayor of London's **Key Worker Living Rent** in Islington; campaign for rent controls
- Help tenants exercise expanded rights under the Renters' Rights Act

### Cost of living
- Continue campaign for **basic income for care leavers** (already pushed through council)
- Use council procurement to support local business and social enterprise; London Living Wage employment; union recognition
- Introduce shared resources: low-rent **'repair café'** spaces; multiple **'Libraries of Things'** (free tool/item lending)
- Joined-up access to benefits and council services

### Climate, air quality and streets
- Reduce traffic, road danger and air pollution; safer streets for walking/wheeling/cycling/play
- **Scrap the green-waste collection charge** to encourage composting
- More street/estate recycling bins
- Drastically reduce incineration of waste
- Joined-up borough net-zero plan: housing retrofit + innovative energy + business decarbonisation
- Promote the "Planetary Health Diet" via council purchasing; expand community gardening and food growing
- Increase tree and wildflower planting; natural flood and river-pollution management
- Protect parks, trees, green spaces — explicit policy not to build on them

### Democracy & governance
- Push council to **reform decision-making** to involve residents directly
- Resident inclusion in scrutiny meetings
- Reform ward partnership meetings into deliberative bodies
- Champion **participatory budgeting**
- Full divestment from oil & gas and from companies "implicated in international aggression and war crimes"
- Local procurement, London Living Wage, union recognition for council suppliers

### Community, equality, safety
- Investment in youth clubs, mental health support, play sessions, accessible/inclusive family activities (especially for SEND children)
- Joined-up social care
- Evidence-based crime reduction; **opposes overpolicing**
- Champion full legal rights and access for trans people, disabled people, older people, young people, migrants and asylum seekers
- Continue Islington as a Borough of Sanctuary
- Stand against discrimination and hatred

## Recent track record (last term, 3 cllrs)
- Successfully campaigned for the council to take the poorest residents out of council tax / arrears
- Pushed council to switch to a more ethical banking provider
- Helped residents with housing repairs; helped set up new residents' associations and community gardens

## Bunhill candidates (Green)

**Alan Clark-Gutiérrez** — Lived in Bunhill ~2.5 years while finishing a Masters in Aerospace Engineering. Young Greens Officer for Islington and Co-Chair of London Young Greens (since Nov 2024). Previously a junior cell engineer and spacecraft design researcher.
> "If elected I would like to scrutinise the council's work on planning, transport and housing committees, plus support fellow councillors who may have less time to read long papers and do the vital admin to keep our Green group punching above its weight."

**Ava Lie** — Moved to Islington in August 2025. Background in International Relations. Joined the Green Party drawn by both local and macro concerns.
> "I believe that true individual wellbeing is inseparable from the health of our local environment, which is why I am committed to building a flourishing community where everyone has the foundations to thrive… In Islington, Green voices are required to push against the Labour Party's complacency."
Local focus: timely, accurate response for those in council housing.

**David Wade** — Chartered Accountant. Has worked across charities, public bodies, a lobbying firm and a local authority. Trustee of an Islington-based social justice charity.
> "I'm pragmatic: change takes persistence, organisation, and listening to people's real concerns…"
Local focus: cleaner streets, safer roads, greener spaces, warm and affordable homes; resolving local issues with residents.
