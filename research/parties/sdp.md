# Social Democratic Party (SDP) — Islington 2026

**Status:** Tiny minor party. The SDP that stands today is the post-1988 continuation of the original Social Democratic Party founded in 1981 by the "Gang of Four". It has had no MPs since the 80s and very limited council representation. Realistically running to be visible, not to win.

**Manifesto:** No public Islington-specific manifesto located. National platform applies.

## What the SDP stands for (national)

The modern SDP positions itself as **economically left, culturally communitarian/conservative** — sometimes called "blue Labour without the Labour":

- Mixed economy; restored industrial policy; sceptical of unrestrained free markets
- Patriotic / pro-nation-state, anti-mass-immigration framing
- Sceptical of identity politics; emphasises common citizenship and class
- Pro-NHS, pro-public services, but tough on welfare conditionality
- Generally green, but skeptical of net-zero costs being borne disproportionately
- Constitutional reform: PR, House of Lords reform
