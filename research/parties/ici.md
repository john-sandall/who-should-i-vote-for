# Islington Community Independents (ICI) — 2026

**Registered party name:** "Islington Community Independents - for the Many" (per the official Statement of Persons Nominated, 10 April 2026).
**Status:** Founded 2024 by four Labour defectors (Ilkay Cinko-Oner, Phil Graham, Matt Nathan, Asima Shaikh). Sits as the joint **Independent & Green Group** under Cllr Benali Hamdache (Green) — i.e. they form the *official opposition* with the Greens. Standing 8 candidates across 6 wards in 2026.

## Why they exist (in their own framing)

Founders cite:
- Labour's response to the war in Gaza / arms sales to Israel
- Labour's treatment of Jeremy Corbyn
- Labour's stance on the two-child benefit cap
- A perceived drift from socialist values
- Concerns about Labour's NHS reform direction
- "Labour's treatment of local representatives" and limits on internal democracy
- Anti-immigration rhetoric they say is incompatible with Islington values

## Stated principles
- "Proud socialists"; "council should be run directly with residents at its core"
- Decisions made *by* communities, not *imposed on* them
- Public money spent for the public good; power shared
- Transparency, accountability, putting people before profit

## Policy areas they say they'll prioritise

(NB: ICI's full "People's Manifesto" PDF was referenced on their site but not retrievable in this research. Positions are drawn from their public web copy and press coverage. Treat as directional.)

- **Housing**: safe, affordable housing; secure decent homes; strong council housing
- **Public services**: protecting NHS; opposing austerity; stronger council services
- **Community safety**: with local accountability
- **Access to culture**
- **International / human rights**: explicit support for Palestinian rights — "demanding freedom for Palestine" — and global justice themes appear prominently in their material
- **Democracy**: more participatory decision-making; resident-led scrutiny
- **Anti-austerity**: oppose central government cuts harming the borough

## Practical record (since defection)

Their councillors have used the opposition platform to:
- Force scrutiny of council decisions in the chamber
- Position publicly on Gaza, NHS, two-child cap
- Function as a numerically small but visible opposition alongside the Greens

## Bunhill candidate (ICI)

**Cllr Phil Graham** — Sitting Bunhill councillor (elected 2018; re-elected as Labour in 2022). **Suspended from Labour in May 2024** for backing Jeremy Corbyn's independent candidacy in Islington North; **formally resigned from Labour on 25 July 2024** over the Commons vote to keep the two-child benefit cap and Labour's withdrawal of the whip from the seven MPs who rebelled. Quoted in the Islington Tribune: *"For Starmer to use starving children as a 'show of strength' is a disgrace."* Sits on 11 council committees (Environment/Climate/Transport Scrutiny, Licensing, etc.) and represents the council on St Sepulchre United Charities. Holds monthly resident surgeries at St Luke's Centre, 90 Central Street.

> Phil Graham is the *only* Bunhill candidate with a public voting and committee record from the current term — that record is the most directly available evidence of how he'd act if re-elected.

## Important context for voters
- The other three ICI defectors (Cinko-Oner, Nathan, Shaikh) are not standing in Bunhill — that race only includes one ICI candidate, Phil Graham.
- A vote for Graham in Bunhill is also (intentionally) a vote that reduces Labour's seat count by one — the seat he previously held was a Labour win.
- ICI does not yet have an extensive local-government track record beyond the current term in opposition.
