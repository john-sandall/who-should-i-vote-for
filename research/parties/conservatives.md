# Conservatives — Islington 2026

**Status:** No councillors currently. Polled 11.2% in 2022 → ~5% projected for 2026. Unlikely to win seats borough-wide.
**Manifesto:** Issued 4 May 2026, 11 pledges (PDF available on the party's site).
**Federation chair:** Nicholas Brainsby. **GLA member:** Cllr Emma Best AM.

## The 11 pledges

1. **Scrap all of Islington's LTNs** (signature, "central" policy)
2. Protect environment and boost public health
3. Cut Council Tax by 5%
4. Improve council housing
5. Encourage new development
6. Encourage responsible landlords
7. Combat crime in our neighbourhoods
8. Repair our streets and improve safety
9. Create more opportunities for our youth
10. Clean up our streets
11. Encourage local business development

## Detail by theme

### LTNs (the headline)
- "We are the only party with all candidates committed to removing the road closures across the borough."
- Argue LTNs increase pollution/congestion on main roads, isolate the vulnerable, and slow emergency response
- If elected to wards: remove all LTNs in those wards immediately
- If not in administration: continue campaign for borough-wide access through remaining LTNs and Blue Badge exemption

### Council tax
- Cut Council Tax by 5%
- Argue Islington's council tax is "about twice what Wandsworth and Westminster charge"
- Comprehensive review of all council spending; "business-like" approach
- Protect core services: weekly bins, street sweeping, fly-tipping, graffiti
- Conservative councillors would reject any allowance increase unless council tax bills come down

### Housing
- **End the Right-to-Buy buyback programme** (claim Islington has spent £89m of its own money on this in last 4 years; argue this moves homes between categories rather than adding any)
- Reallocate that budget to **maintenance** of lifts, heating, CCTV, etc.
- Insulate all council housing
- Work with conservation officer for green retrofits in conservation areas (~half of Islington)
- Solar panels on all new social housing
- **Cut the affordable housing target from 50% → 35%** to "make stalled projects financially viable"
- Helpline for "accidental landlords"; grace period on licensing fees for new single-property landlords
- "Innocent until proven guilty" approach to landlord enforcement (still pursue rogue operators)

### Environment
- Help energy companies identify fuel-poor / vulnerable households for ECO 4 efficiency works
- Waive resident parking charge for electric cars; charging points in all new lampposts
- Council car-pooling scheme
- All new council buildings hit Net Zero; retrofit older buildings where practical
- More trees, hedges, green walls, vegetable plots
- Mandate green spaces as part of all new housing
- Solar power and LED lighting on council estate
- More bike storage facilities
- Press the Mayor to ban diesel buses through Islington

### Crime
- "Bobbies on Bikes" patrol model
- Use Government's Safer Streets Fund for lighting and CCTV in hot spots
- Residents to know local officers' names
- Schools-and-police education on knife crime
- Bleed kits on main streets, with first aid training

### Streets
- Repair trip hazards; restore pavements after works
- Install drop kerbs (e.g. Highbury Corner)
- Tackle street clutter (A-frame boards, badly placed signs)
- Address pavements deformed by tree roots

### Youth
- Discount on business rates for hiring 16/18-year-old school leavers (with training/apprenticeships)
- Repurpose schools, community centres, leisure centres for youth leisure & skills
- "Your Future" careers guidance from Year 7

### Cleaning
- Replace estate refuse collection with smart, deep-welled bins (collection triggered when nearly full)
- Re-introduce **free collection of bulky household items**

### Business
- Free parking for less able-bodied residents; limited free parking elsewhere
- Business coaches, hot desks in underused council property
- Targeted business rate waivers, loans, grants
