# Reform UK — Islington 2026

**Status:** No councillors currently; first time fielding a serious slate in Islington. Polled ~7.9% projected for 2026 — modest borough share but new vote on the right.
**Manifesto:** **No local Islington manifesto issued.** Reform UK's local-government strategy nationally relies on national policy lines and social media campaigning rather than local manifestos.

## What's known about Reform's pitch

Because Reform have not issued an Islington manifesto, voters effectively get the **national platform** as policy proxy:

- Hard-line on illegal immigration; smaller, more targeted state
- Large tax cuts paired with some interventionist industrial measures
- Tougher criminal justice / law and order
- Rollback of net-zero / climate policy
- Cost-of-living focus

Reform candidates standing in Islington tend to campaign on:
- Council tax / value for money concerns
- Anti-LTN sentiment (overlap with Conservatives)
- Crime and policing
- "Wasteful" council spending narrative

## Caveats

- Of all the parties contesting Bunhill, Reform UK has the **least transparent local policy platform** because no local manifesto exists. The party's national policy is published and available, but specific Islington commitments are not.
- One source (NW Londoner) noted "Reform UK have not released a local branch manifesto, though their national policies include tackling the cost-of-living crisis, illegal immigration and crime levels."
- Independent of borough strategy, Reform's national leadership has had several high-profile policy reversals and intra-party disputes during 2025.
