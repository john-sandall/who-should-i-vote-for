# Labour — Islington 2026

**Status:** Incumbent. Has run Islington since 2010. Won 48 of 51 seats in 2022.
**Manifesto:** "Five missions" — 40+ pages. [Full text](https://islington-labour.org.uk/islington-labours-2026-manifesto/)
**Borough leadership:** Cllr Una O'Halloran (Council Leader since November 2024 and Group Leader; Caledonian ward; first council leader to be a council tenant; first round leadership win)

## The five missions

1. Decent, secure, genuinely affordable homes for every resident
2. A safer, cleaner, healthier borough
3. Tackle cost of living and support local business
4. A great place to grow up and grow old
5. Continue delivering for residents and empower diverse communities

## Housing
- Spend £58m buying back ex-RTB homes (Conservatives say £89m has already been spent on this since 2022)
- Maintain 50% affordable in new developments (Conservatives want this cut to 35%)
- Invest £388m in existing council stock 2026–30; 100% decent homes by 2030
- WhatsApp repairs reporting service rolled out borough-wide
- Named housing officers serving max 550 residents each
- Expand selective landlord licensing; campaign for rent controls
- Investigate illegal short-term lets (Airbnb-type); max council tax on second/empty homes
- Maintain "Housing First" approach; reduce time in temporary accommodation
- Pension fund divestment from companies profiting from "conflict, genocide, human rights abuses"

## Safety
- Launch £1.9m Safer Communities fund (resident-led)
- Expand 24/7 neighbourhood patrols to estates and public spaces
- "Three strikes" approach to anti-social behaviour
- Targeted PSPOs at Finsbury Park, Old Street, Archway
- Night Angels scheme (trained volunteers in town centres)
- New borough VAWG strategy

## Environment & transport
- 400 new EV charging points 2026–30
- Pension fund net-zero by 2035
- More solar on council buildings; expand Bunhill Heat & Power-style local energy networks
- Pothole fix in 72 hours; £12m extra for roads/pavements
- More School Streets, more cycle hangars, more rain gardens & street trees
- Continue weekly bin collections; clean streets 4× per week; remove fly-tips within 48 hours

## Cost of living
- 1,000 into work and 1,000 into apprenticeships
- 1/3 of £650m annual contracts to local suppliers by 2027
- Continue one of the country's most generous Council Tax Support schemes (£28m, ~24,500 households)
- New targeted council tax support for households with terminal illness
- Launch Islington Poverty Truth Commission

## Children & young people
- Universal free school meals (primary)
- Year 7 laptop/tablet for every child
- 11-by-11 cultural programme; 100 hours work experience by age 16
- Top-25%-in-London exam target; reduce attainment gap
- Continue investing in playgrounds and youth services

## Older people
- "Six for over-60s" free/low-cost cultural and leisure offer
- Free over-60s swimming and tennis
- Establish an Islington Intergenerational Fairness Commission

## Council services
- "Islington In Touch" guarantee: routine requests resolved or planned within 10 days
- Maintain Access Islington Hubs
- Pilot AI to improve services and staff efficiency
- Continue "in-source first" approach
- Multi-million Sobell Centre refurb; Barnard Park upgrade; new library at Vorley Road, Archway

## Headline criticism aimed at the manifesto
- Greens: claim 18 new private homes are being built for every 1 council home in current pipeline; argue public land has been given up for unaffordable luxury homes
- Lib Dems: call out council's repairs failures (Housing Ombudsman special report); flag tube fare rises and "fleecehold" charges to leaseholders
- Conservatives: argue council tax is among the highest in London (~2× Wandsworth/Westminster) and 50% affordable target deters developers
- ICI: argue Labour has drifted from socialist values, blames Labour-led council/Labour government for funding cuts

## Bunhill candidates (Labour)

**Cllr Valerie Bossman-Quarshie** — Lifelong Islingtonian. Councillor since 2021 (won Bunhill 2022 with the highest vote share in the ward). YMCA Trustee, St Luke's Community Trust Trustee. School governor at Moreland and St Luke's CoE. Focus: opportunities for families and high-quality education.

**Cllr Troy Gallagher** — 25 years in the Bunhill community. Long-serving councillor. Islington's first Irish-born Mayor. Says he has championed social housing programmes, introduced a lettings policy for resident stability, and expanded youth investment.

**Kiran Prasad** — 25-year Bunhill resident. Primary school governor and trustee of a local volunteering charity. Says she's championed tenants' rights and challenged unfair service charges. Works on national children's policy / care-in-community research. Replacing Phil Graham on the Labour ticket.
