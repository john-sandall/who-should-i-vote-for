# Liberal Democrats — Islington 2026

**Status:** No councillors currently (lost their last seat in 2010). Predicted ~2 seats in 2026 (PollCheck).
**Manifesto:** "Working for Islington" (10 pledges, 2026) building on 2022's "Listening to Islington".
**Local lead:** Kate Pothalingam (Party Chair), Nick Wakeling (spokesperson).

## 2026 framing
> "Our common sense focus on getting basic services right, protecting the environment and ensuring value for money is more important than ever." — Kate Pothalingam, Party Chair

The 2026 pledge document itself is brief — a 459-word web piece — explicitly building on the 2022 manifesto rather than replacing it. The four headline themes per the *Islington Gazette* are:

## Headline 2026 pledges

### Housing
- Build **1,000 new social rent homes** on disused sites
- Improve council repairs programme
- Repair / replace all council lifts over 30 years old
- Immediate fix of "ongoing issues" — heating, leaks, structural damage
- Better maintenance of estate communal areas

### Crime / policing
- Fund extra police officers
- Clampdown on moped and knife crime via increased police presence
- Save police station counter services

### Environment
- Air pollution monitors at every primary school
- Bike Hire Charter (pricing, parking, redistribution)

### EU / civic rights
- Reverse council fees for EU citizenship applications, opposing the recent 33% hike

### Existing campaigns (still live)
- "Fix Our Lifts" — borough-wide audit, 30-year replacement target
- Bike Hire Charter
- Better Repairs for Council Tenants and Leaseholders
- Save Our Police Stations Counter Services

## Carried over from the 2022 manifesto (still party policy)

The Lib Dems explicitly say they "stand by" their 2022 *Listening to Islington* manifesto. Selected highlights:

**Cost of living**
- Never raise council tax above inflation
- Help leaseholders meet building works costs
- Cut energy bills via home efficiency

**Climate**
- Energy efficiency programme for all council homes
- Re-open the Green Living Centre (closed by Labour) as a one-stop retrofit shop
- Double the borough's tree count (50+ new trees per ward per year, target 750/year)

**LTNs (a notable middle-ground position)**
- Support LTNs *in principle*
- Citizens Assemblies in each existing LTN before any change
- No new LTNs without prior Citizens Assembly endorsement
- Immediate exemptions for Blue Badge holders, carers, essential users
- (NB: Labour later did concede Blue Badge exemptions in Jan 2026)

**Transport**
- Step-free access at every Islington station
- Cut secure bike storage to £20 (parity with car parking)
- Cycle hangar on every street/estate where 6+ households request one
- Protected cycle routes on all busy roads

**Air quality**
- Make Clean Air a dedicated cabinet portfolio
- Sensors and "green screens" outside schools and nurseries
- Diesel-free borough by 2030; oppose Edmonton incinerator

**Cleaner streets**
- Double recycling rates (claim Islington is below London and England averages)
- More street sweepers, more bins
- Crackdown on fly-tipping (claim it rose 23% under Labour)

**Housing**
- Build 200 new council homes per year (1,000 over the term)
- Review garage/car park sites for housing without losing greenspace
- Cap Holloway Prison redevelopment at 700 homes

**Safety**
- More local police; Victims Commissioner
- Safety audits of every estate
- Safe space for young people in every neighbourhood and large estate

## Track record / context
- Held Bunhill 1998–2006; lost it to Labour 2010
- Have been the loudest opposition voice on the **Housing Ombudsman special report** (2023, finding "severe maladministration" at 24.7% — 4× national average)
- Campaigning on EU citizenship fees, "fleecehold" leaseholder charges, ZipCar withdrawal, tube fare rises
