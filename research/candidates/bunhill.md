# Bunhill Ward — All 16 Candidates

**Election:** Thursday 7 May 2026, polls 07:00–22:00
**Seats:** 3 — voters cast up to 3 votes
**2022 turnout:** 30.3% (lowest in Islington)
**Currently held by:** 3 Labour councillors elected 2022 (Bossman-Quarshie, Gallagher, Graham). Graham was suspended from Labour May 2024 (for backing Corbyn's independent campaign) and resigned 25 July 2024 over the two-child cap vote; he stands again as ICI.

**Polling stations (per Statement of Persons Nominated, 10 Apr 2026):**
- Vibast Community Centre, 167 Old Street (registers SBUA-1 to SBUA-3361)
- Tompion Hall Community Centre, 40 Percival Street (register SBUB)
- St Luke's Community Centre, 90 Central Street (register SBUC)

The candidates are listed alphabetically by surname (the same order they will appear on the ballot paper). Candidate-specific details below come from the official Statement of Persons Nominated where available.

## How the seats may fall (rough)
- Three Labour incumbents-of-record (one has defected) means Labour will defend three seats with two incumbents on the ballot — a slightly weaker posture than 2022.
- Greens have polled strongly across Islington (~34% projected vs 22% in 2022) and field a full slate.
- The strongest "minor" candidate is Phil Graham (ICI) — name recognition, full sitting-councillor record.
- Lib Dems hope for one borough seat (PollCheck projects ~2 across Islington); they have not historically been competitive in Bunhill since 2010.
- Conservatives, Reform, SDP polling far behind in Bunhill given borough trends; local upset would be surprising.
